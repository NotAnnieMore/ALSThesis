\section*{Abstract}
\label{sec:abstract}

%Drug discovery is a highly time-consuming, complex, and expensive process with low rates of success. Recent estimates point that to launch a new drug, an average \$1.8 billion and twelve years are required \cite{sinha2018drug}. This is partly due to the high dimensional of the chemical space, which has been estimated to include between $10^{33}$ and $10^{60}$  synthetically accessible molecules \cite{polishchuk2013estimation}. Evaluating the entire chemical space is prohibitively expensive, so it is of the utmost importance to find ways of narrowing down the search space.
