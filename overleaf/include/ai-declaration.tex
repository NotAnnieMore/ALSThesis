\chapter*{Declaration on the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence}
\markboth{Declaration on the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence}{}

Generative artificial intelligence tools were used during the preparation of this dissertation, namely ChatGPT, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot Pro (including Claude models), Google NotebookLM and Google Gemini. These tools supported preliminary exploration of topics and sources, language revision and text restructuring, code review and development, and refinement of the graphical presentation of selected results.

These tools were not treated as independent sources of scientific evidence or as substitutes for methodological judgement. Their outputs were reviewed and edited by the author, and relevant claims and references were checked against the original sources. The author also verified the numerical results and code included in the dissertation and made the final decisions concerning the analyses and their interpretation. The author assumes full responsibility for the content of this work.
